1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Matchcard (iOS bundle identifier com.cay.SportsApp), developed by Cay Weissman ("we," "us," or "the developer"), handles information when you use the Matchcard mobile application (the "App").

Related source repositories: Matchcard and SportsApp .

2. What Matchcard does

Matchcard is a native iOS sports application providing live scores, schedules, standings, statistics, news, player information, and related sports content.

Matchcard supports the NFL, college football, NBA, college basketball, and MLB.

Features may include ESPN Analytics win-probability charts, customizable Home widgets, pinned games, Lock Screen Live Activities, and live game discussions.

Users can browse sports information without creating an account. Certain interactive features, including live game chat, may require Sign in with Apple.

No betting or gambling. Matchcard does not provide sports betting, wagering, sportsbook odds, deposits, gambling payouts, or gambling services. Win-probability charts are informational sports analytics and are not betting odds.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information collected through app features

The information processed depends on which Matchcard features you choose to use.

When you use Sign in with Apple or live game chat, information processed may include:

  • An account identifier provided through Sign in with Apple
  • Your name or email address, if provided through Apple's authentication service
  • An Apple private relay email address, if you choose Hide My Email
  • Chat messages, replies, mentions, and reactions
  • Message timestamps and associated account identifiers
  • User reports, moderation information, and blocking preferences

Information necessary to operate live chat and moderation may be transmitted to and stored by the services supporting those features.

3.2 Information not required for basic use

Browsing scores, schedules, statistics, standings, and news does not require you to provide your name or email address directly to Matchcard.

Matchcard does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or Health data for its core sports features.

Matchcard does not require precise location permissions to display sports information.

3.3 Technical information

When the App communicates with external services, technical information such as IP addresses, request times, device characteristics, and network requests may be processed by those services.

This information may be used to deliver content, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and support security.

3.4 Apple platform information

Apple may independently process App Store downloads, purchase records, authentication information, diagnostics, and crash reports according to Apple's privacy practices.

4. Sign in with Apple

Matchcard offers Sign in with Apple for features requiring user authentication, including live game chat.

Apple may provide Matchcard with a unique account identifier and, depending on your choices and Apple's available information, your name or email address.

If you choose Hide My Email, Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your personal email address.

Authentication information is used to identify your account, associate your activity with that account, and support account security and moderation.

Signing in is optional for users who only want to browse sports information.

Matchcard does not receive your Apple Account password through Sign in with Apple.

You can manage Matchcard's Sign in with Apple authorization through your Apple Account settings.

Revoking authorization does not necessarily delete account information or chat content already stored by Matchcard. To request deletion of stored account information, contact us using the details below.

5. Live game chat

Matchcard includes live discussions associated with sporting events.

Users may participate in conversations using messages, replies, mentions, and reactions.

Chat content is user-generated information. Messages and other interactions may be visible to other people using the corresponding game discussion.

Information associated with chat activity may include:

  • Message content
  • Replies and mentions
  • Reactions
  • Account identifiers
  • Game or discussion identifiers
  • Message creation times
  • Moderation-related information

Chat information may be stored and transmitted through the services used to operate live discussions.

Other participants may read, copy, screenshot, or otherwise retain content that you choose to post.

Public chat notice. Do not share passwords, financial information, private contact details, or other sensitive information in live game discussions.

Matchcard may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to enforce community rules, investigate reports, or protect users.

6. Reporting and blocking

Matchcard provides tools for reporting inappropriate content and blocking users.

When a report is submitted, information related to the report may be processed, including the reported content, relevant account identifiers, the reason for the report, and associated discussion details.

This information is used to review possible violations, investigate abuse, and determine whether moderation action is appropriate.

Blocking information may be stored to maintain your blocking preferences and apply restrictions within chat.

Blocking another user does not necessarily delete messages previously submitted by either person.

Moderation records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

7. Third-party services

7.1 Sports data providers

Matchcard retrieves sports information from third-party sources, including ESPN endpoints and related sports data services.

This information may include scores, schedules, statistics, standings, player profiles, news, and win-probability analytics.

Network requests may expose technical information such as IP addresses, request timing, and device characteristics to the providers receiving those requests.

Matchcard is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ESPN, the NFL, NBA, MLB, NCAA, or their respective organizations.

7.2 Authentication and chat services

Matchcard uses Apple authentication services for Sign in with Apple.

The services supporting live chat and moderation may process the account, message, and technical information necessary to provide those features.

Such processing is subject to the applicable service arrangements and privacy practices.

7.3 App Store services

Apple provides App Store distribution, authentication, and in-app purchase infrastructure.

Apple's independent processing is governed by its own privacy policy.

8. Local device storage

Matchcard stores certain preferences and settings on your device to remember your choices and improve performance.

These may be stored using standard iOS storage mechanisms, including UserDefaults, AppStorage, and local application files.

Locally stored information may include:

  • Appearance preferences
  • Default opening screen or league
  • Hide Scores settings
  • Followed or favorited teams
  • Pinned games
  • Custom Home widget configurations
  • Cached sports information
  • Other interface preferences

Some settings associated with authenticated features may also be processed by the services supporting those features.

Removing the App generally removes its local application data according to iOS storage behavior, but does not automatically guarantee deletion of information stored remotely.

9. Home widgets and Live Activities

Matchcard supports customizable Home widgets displaying information about teams, players, leaderboards, and news.

Users may also pin games and enable Live Activities that present game information on the Lock Screen and other supported iOS interfaces.

Widget configurations and selected games may be stored locally to maintain your preferred layout and displayed content.

Sports information displayed through widgets and Live Activities may be retrieved from external sports data providers.

These features may also use Apple system services to display or update information.

Content displayed in widgets or Live Activities may be visible to anyone who can view your device screen.

Availability and behavior depend on device compatibility, iOS settings, and network connectivity.

10. Optional tips and custom app icons

Matchcard offers optional in-app purchases allowing users to support development and unlock custom app icons.

These purchases are processed through Apple's App Store payment infrastructure.

Matchcard does not directly receive your complete payment card number or payment credentials through Apple's in-app purchase system.

Information about purchase status, purchased products, and transaction identifiers may be processed to confirm purchases, restore eligible purchases, and provide purchased content.

Payment information independently collected by Apple is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Optional tips and icon purchases are not required to access basic sports scores and information.

11. Children's privacy

Matchcard is a general-audience sports information application and is not directed at children under the age of 13.

Matchcard does not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13.

Because live game chat permits user-generated content and account-based interaction, users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public discussions.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through Matchcard, contact us.

We will review the request and take appropriate steps under applicable law.

12. Data retention

Local preferences and cached information may remain on your device until you modify them, clear application data, or uninstall Matchcard.

Information associated with authenticated features, including account identifiers, chat activity, and moderation records, may be retained as needed to operate those features.

Retention may also be necessary for security, abuse prevention, dispute resolution, and compliance with applicable legal requirements.

Deleting the App does not automatically delete remotely stored account or chat information.

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their Matchcard account using the contact information below.

Certain information may need to be retained for legitimate security or legal reasons, even after a deletion request.

13. Sharing and selling of information

Matchcard does not sell personal information to data brokers or advertising companies.

Matchcard does not use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information may be processed or disclosed in the following circumstances:

  • Public chat messages and interactions are visible to other users.
  • Authentication services process information needed for Sign in with Apple.
  • Chat infrastructure processes information necessary to deliver conversations and moderation features.
  • Sports data providers receive technical network requests necessary to retrieve content.
  • Apple processes information related to App Store purchases and platform functionality.
  • Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or valid legal process.

Matchcard does not include third-party advertising as part of its described version 1.1 features.

14. Security

Matchcard aims to use reasonable security measures appropriate to the information processed through the App.

Network requests for sports information use HTTPS.

Sign in with Apple provides authentication through Apple's identity infrastructure.

Account-based features and moderation tools are intended to help protect the integrity of live discussions.

No internet-connected service or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Users are encouraged to keep iOS updated, protect their Apple Account, and avoid sharing sensitive information publicly.
