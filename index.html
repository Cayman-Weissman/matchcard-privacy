1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Matchcard (iOS bundle identifier com.cay.SportsApp), developed by Cayman Weissman (“we,” “us,” or “the developer”), handles information when you use the Matchcard mobile application (the “App”).

Related source repositories (for context only; this policy governs the App): Matchcard and SportsApp.

2. What Matchcard does

Matchcard is a native SwiftUI iOS app that displays National Football League (NFL) and college football (CFB) scores, schedules, statistics, news, and related sports information. Where available, the App may show ESPN Analytics win-probability information. Matchcard is a scores and information app only.

No betting or gambling. Matchcard does not offer betting, gambling, wagering, sportsbook odds, deposits, payouts, or related features. Sportsbook-style odds have been removed. Any win-probability figures shown are informational analytics only and are not wagers or odds for gambling.

The App does not provide user accounts, sign-in, guest profiles, social login, or cloud-synced user profiles.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information we do not collect by default

By design, Matchcard does not require you to create an account and does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses
  • Account credentials, usernames, or passwords
  • Payment or financial information
  • Precise location data for our own use
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or Health data
  • Advertising identifiers for third-party advertising networks (the App does not include third-party ads)

We do not operate a backend that stores personal profiles for Matchcard users. We do not sell personal information.

3.2 Information processed on your device

Preferences and favorites you set in the App are stored locally on your device (see Local device storage). That data stays on your device unless you choose to share it yourself (for example, via iOS system features outside the App’s control).

3.3 Information we do not collect via analytics SDKs

As of the effective date of this policy, Matchcard does not integrate a third-party analytics SDK (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar) and does not include third-party advertising SDKs. If that changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when such features ship.

3.4 Information Apple may process

When you download or use the App through the Apple App Store or Apple platforms, Apple may collect information according to Apple’s own privacy practices (for example, App Store account data, device diagnostics you share with Apple, or crash reports if you enable them). That processing is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, not this one.

4. Third-party data sources (ESPN and similar)

Matchcard fetches publicly available sports data—such as scores, schedules, team and player statistics, news headlines, and related content—from ESPN’s public APIs and related endpoints over HTTPS. ESPN and any other content providers are independent third parties.

When the App requests this data, standard network information may be visible to those providers or to network intermediaries in the ordinary course of HTTPS requests (for example, IP address, approximate location inferred by the network, device/user-agent characteristics, request timing, and requested URLs). We do not control ESPN’s or other providers’ independent collection or use of such technical data. Please review ESPN’s privacy policy and terms for how they handle information.

Matchcard displays third-party content for informational purposes. Trademarks, logos, team names, and media belong to their respective owners.

5. Local device storage

Matchcard stores certain settings and preferences on your device so the App can remember your choices between launches. This typically uses iOS mechanisms such as UserDefaults / @AppStorage, and may use on-device file storage (for example via FileManager) to cache directory or content data for performance and offline-friendly use.

Examples of locally stored preferences include:

  • Appearance (for example light, dark, or system)
  • Open To (which screen or league to open by default)
  • Hide Scores (spoiler protection preferences)
  • Followed or favorited teams you choose in the App

This local data is not uploaded to a Matchcard-operated server because Matchcard does not maintain user accounts or a sync backend for preferences. Uninstalling the App, or clearing the App’s data through iOS settings where available, removes local preferences and caches according to how iOS manages app storage.

6. Network access

Matchcard requires network access to retrieve live and recent sports information. Requests are made over HTTPS to third-party sports data endpoints (see Third-party data sources). Without network access, some features may be unavailable or limited to any on-device cached content.

7. Children’s privacy (COPPA)

Matchcard is a general-audience sports scores app. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not provide account registration that would solicit personal information from children.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us in a way that relates to Matchcard, please contact us using the details below and we will take reasonable steps to delete such information if we have it.

8. Data retention

Because Matchcard does not operate user accounts or a personal-data backend for the App, we do not retain personal profiles for Matchcard users on our servers. Preferences and caches remain on your device until you change them, clear app data, or uninstall the App.

Temporary network logs or similar technical records, if any are created by our hosting of this Privacy Policy website or by GitHub Pages infrastructure, are subject to the practices of those hosting providers and are not used by us to identify App users.

9. Sharing and selling of information

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share Matchcard user personal information with third parties for their independent marketing because we do not collect such personal information through the App by default. Limited technical information may be processed by:

  • Third-party sports data providers when the App fetches public sports content
  • Apple, in connection with distribution and platform services
  • Service providers that host this Privacy Policy page (for example GitHub Pages), solely to deliver the webpage

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security, to the limited extent such information is available to us.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the App. Network requests for sports data use HTTPS. Preferences are stored using standard iOS on-device storage APIs. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security of information processed on your device or in transit to third-party services.

You can further protect your device with a passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, and by keeping iOS and the App updated.

11. Your privacy rights (including California / CCPA-style rights)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information, such as the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to correct inaccurate personal information, the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information (as those terms are defined under applicable law), and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights.

How this applies to Matchcard: Because Matchcard does not create accounts and does not collect personal information by default for App operation, there is typically little or no personal information held by us about you as a Matchcard user. Preferences live on your device; you can change or delete them in the App or by removing the App.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
