About Matchcard

Matchcard is an iOS app for NFL and college football (CFB) scores and related game information. It does not include betting features and does not require an account to use.

How to get help

For bug reports, feature requests, or general questions about Matchcard, email the developer:

  • Email: caymanweissman@gmail.com

Please include your iOS version, device model, and a short description of the problem when reporting a bug.

Accounts & in-app support

Matchcard has no user accounts, sign-in, or in-app messaging. We cannot reset passwords, recover account data, or provide account-based support, because none of those features exist in the app.

Preferences and local settings live on your device. Removing the app clears that local data.
